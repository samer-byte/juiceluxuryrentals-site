Before you text
Straight answers.

    

      
Who am I texting?
Juice — the founder, not a ticket queue. Based in DC, booking Miami year-round. Every villa, car and boat on this site comes from operators we work with directly; you get one person and one thread from the first text to the last drop-off.

      
Why no prices on the site?
Villas, cars and boats price by date, length of stay and season — a February championship weekend and a quiet Tuesday in September are not the same number. We quote your exact dates so the number you see is the number you pay.

      
Are the photos real?
Yes — every villa page is the operator's full folder for that exact house, and the cars and boats are the ones you get. If a specific property is out for your dates we say so and show the alternatives.

      
How does payment work?
A 50% deposit locks the booking; the balance is due before arrival. Cars require a valid license, full-coverage insurance and a security deposit set by the operator.

      
Do you only do Miami?
Miami is home base for villas and cars. Yacht charters run in Miami and on the Potomac in Washington, DC. Tables, dinners and drivers can be arranged in both cities.

      
How fast do you reply?
Personally, usually within hours, always within a day. Curated options with photos within 24 hours of your dates.
