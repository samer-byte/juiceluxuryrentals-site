Yacht charters in Miami & DC

Biscayne Bay by day, the skyline by night — captained, stocked, sized to your group.

Four-hour charters are the standard; sunset and midnight slots book fastest. Most boats carry 13 guests under Coast Guard rules, and the 100-footers take groups of 30 to 50 for the big nights. Captain and crew included, pickup from Brickell and Miami Beach marinas, jet skis by the hour to match. Every boat comes through operators we work with directly, and they're sized for the big nights: July 4th link-ups, birthdays, multi-yacht fireworks.
